# 🚗 My Favorite Movie: *Cars*

![Cars Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/3/34/Cars_2006.jpg)

## 🏁 Overview
**Cars** is a 2006 animated film produced by *Pixar Animation Studios* and released by *Walt Disney Pictures*.  
It follows the story of **Lightning McQueen**, a hotshot race car who learns that *life isn’t just about racing — it’s about friendship and humility.*

> *"Life is a journey. Enjoy the drive!"* – Doc Hudson

---

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Lightning McQueen** – The ambitious rookie race car.
- **Mater** – The funny and loyal tow truck.
- **Sally Carrera** – The smart and kind Porsche.
- **Doc Hudson** – The wise mentor and former race champion.

---

## 🏎️ Why I Love *Cars*
1. The animation is **stunning** and full of detail.
2. The *message about teamwork and growth* is inspiring.
3. The soundtrack, especially *“Life Is a Highway”*, is unforgettable!
4. The world-building of Radiator Springs feels nostalgic and heartwarming.

---

## 🔧 Fun Fact
The number **95** on Lightning McQueen’s side is a tribute to the release year of Pixar’s first film, *Toy Story (1995)*.
